Eco-friendly approach to business mobile phone recycling

It's in good hands, if it's handheld

We know how important it is for your team to have the latest tech when showcasing your brand. Unfortunately, old devices just don't cut it, so we'll recycle any phone, tablet or handheld device used for business, no matter the make, model or condition.

3 simple steps to secure your data before recycling

✓

Back up important files, such as documents and photos, and transfer them to your new device if required or back up in the cloud.

✓

Sign out of all accounts on your device, that means all apps for example expense software, apps used for communication, approvals etc.

✓

Remove any SIM or memory cards if possible, although these can also be recycled.

We also recycle EPOS systems

EPOS systems streamline the retail sales process, but once they reach the end of their life, they can create a lot of e-waste. The good news is we can responsibly recycle all the connected EPOS hardware of any brand (e.g. Epson, Zebra, Honeywell, Datalogic, Star Micronics, ELO etc.), including monitors, customer display readouts, receipt printers, PDQ terminals, keyboards, barcode scanners, and cash drawers to keep waste to a minimum and disposal eco-friendly.

Frequently asked questions

Is mobile phone and tablet recycling free for businesses?

Yes, mobile devices are included in our free collection list – you’ll need to recycle 10 devices altogether to qualify for a free collection.

What should I do before recycling a mobile device?

Back up anything you need, sign out of all accounts and apps, and remove any SIM or memory cards where possible, although these can also be recycled with us.

Do you recycle EPOS systems and accessories?

Yes. We recycle EPOS hardware of any brand, including monitors, receipt printers, PDQ terminals, barcode scanners and cash drawers, alongside phones and tablets.

Do you securely destroy data on mobile devices?

Yes. We use NCSC-approved data erasure software and CPNI-approved shredding technology to destroy any confidential data on data-bearing devices. Read more about our secure data destruction process.

What happens to old mobile phone device batteries?

Batteries are managed and recycled separately from the rest of the device, to reduce the risk of fire and to comply with hazardous waste regulations.

Why is reusing an old phone or tablet better than recycling it?

A working device that’s refurbished and redeployed avoids the energy, water and raw materials needed to manufacture a replacement. We assess every mobile device for reuse before it’s considered for recycling.

Can you collect mobile devices alongside other business IT equipment?

Yes. We regularly collect phones, tablets and EPOS hardware together with laptops, computers, monitors and other redundant IT equipment.
