Responsible server recycling

Servers contain a wide range of valuable materials and components that can be recovered, reused or recycled. By processing redundant equipment responsibly, we help reduce unnecessary waste and the demand for new raw materials. We collect a wide range of server equipment, including Tower Servers, Rack Servers, Blade Servers, server chassis, motherboards, CPUs, RAM, RAID controllers, hard drives, SSDs, storage arrays, power supplies and network cards. Where equipment is suitable, components can be recovered for reuse, while materials such as copper, aluminium, steel and precious metals can be recovered through responsible recycling. Our aim is to maximise reuse and material recovery wherever possible while keeping waste to a minimum.

Secure server data destruction

Servers can contain large amounts of sensitive business, customer and employee information. When equipment reaches the end of its useful life, ensuring the data stored on it is securely dealt with is essential.

SavingTech provides a secure IT asset disposal process that protects your equipment and data throughout the recycling journey. Storage media can be securely erased or physically destroyed where required, with data destruction documentation and certificates available to provide a clear audit trail.

Once your servers have been securely processed, we can also manage your wider IT recycling requirements. From computers and laptops to monitors, printers and networking equipment, SavingTech provides a complete ITAD and WEEE recycling solution for businesses.

Frequently asked questions

Is server recycling free for businesses?

Yes. We collect and recycle any volume of tower, rack or blade servers free of charge, and free collection applies when you recycle 10 or more qualifying IT assets.

Why should I recycle my old servers?

Recycling helps you meet your WEEE responsibilities, protect sensitive data and recover valuable materials for reuse and recycling.

Do you securely destroy data on servers?

Yes. We securely erase or physically destroy data-bearing drives before equipment is reused or recycled, with certificates available for completed data destruction.

What types of servers do you collect?

We collect tower, rack and blade servers, along with server chassis, CPUs, RAM, hard drives, SSDs, storage arrays, power supplies and other server components.

Can I get a certificate for data destruction?

Yes. We can provide data destruction certificates to give you a clear record of how your data-bearing equipment was securely processed.

Can you collect servers with other IT equipment?

Yes. We can collect servers alongside computers, laptops, monitors, networking equipment, printers and other redundant business IT equipment.
