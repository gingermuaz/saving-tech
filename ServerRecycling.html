Give your old server components a new life

Our server recycling service can help you to stay compliant and secure. We collect and recycle any volume of Tower Servers, Rack Servers, or Blade Servers free of charge – nationwide. Whatever the size of your company, we'll help you manage your corporate IT disposal with compliance and the environment in mind.

Servers must be recycled by law, in line with the WEEE regulations.

Servers are complex machines which contain a range of valuable materials. However these can also be harmful to the environment, polluting soil and water if not disposed of properly.

We recycle servers responsibly, reusing parts like processors and memory which cuts down on the need for raw materials, saving energy and reducing the environmental impact of making new devices.

Did you know that you can recycle all components of servers and networking hardware? This includes motherboards, cards, and CPUs, which contain recoverable metals like copper, silver, gold, and silicon. Similarly, we'll recycle the hard drives & storage arrays, cables, power supplies, and optical drives. Even the metal and plastic cases can be repurposed. Secure server data destruction

Protecting confidential customer, employee and business data is critical, as unauthorised access could lead to financial losses, reputational damage, and regulatory fines. Our reliable, secure process ensures the safe handling and destruction of all sensitive data stored on servers at all stages of recycling. Plus, we can take away all your other IT recycling including computers, laptops, monitors and printers. We provide an all-in-one WEEE recycling solution.

Our certified and monitored process ensures the secure management and destruction of all sensitive data stored on equipment during collection, transit, and in our facilities, before it's broken down or recycled.

We use the latest NCSC-approved data erasure software and CPNI-approved shredding technology to ensure the secure destruction of any confidential data stored on your computer is properly destroyed. You can be confident your IT recycling is in safe hands.

Frequently asked questions

Is server recycling free for businesses?

Yes. We collect and recycle any volume of tower, rack or blade servers free of charge, and free collection applies when you recycle 10 or more qualifying IT assets.

Why do I need to recycle my old servers?

Servers must be recycled by law in line with the WEEE regulations. In addition, recycling your old servers protects your business from data breaches, prevents toxic electronic waste from harming the environment, and recovers valuable raw materials.

Do you securely destroy data on servers before recycling?

Yes. We use NCSC-approved data erasure software and CPNI-approved shredding technology to destroy sensitive data held on servers and storage arrays. Read more about our secure data destruction process.

What types of servers and networking equipment do you collect?

We collect rackmount, blade, high-density and freestanding servers, along with switches, routers, firewalls, UPS units and other networking and power management equipment. See our full list of what we collect.

Do you provide a certificate of destruction for servers?

Yes. All collections receive a Waste Transfer Note plus a Carbon Offset Certificate. You can also request our enhanced Certificate of Destruction for each individual device.

Can you collect servers alongside other data centre equipment?

Yes. If you're decommissioning a larger data centre or server room, our data centre IT recycling service covers the full range of associated hardware in one project.

How quickly can you collect decommissioned servers?

We typically collect within 24 to 72 hours of your enquiry, with full tracking across our fleet for an audited collection.
