2. The information we collect

We collect information you give us when you:

  • Book a collection, order Saving Bins or arrange to send equipment by post: your name, company name, email address, phone number, collection or delivery postcode, billing address, details of the items you want to recycle, any asset list you upload and anything you write in the questions box.
  • Book secure data destruction or a laptop collection: the same contact details, plus the service you're interested in.
  • Join our email list: your first name and email address.
  • Phone us: your name, contact details and a note of what we discuss.

We don't ask for sensitive information, such as health details, so please don't include it in our forms.

Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub records your IP address when you visit, to keep the service secure. We don't use this to identify you.

4. Data on the equipment you give us

Computers, phones, drives and other equipment often still hold data, which may include personal information about your staff or customers. We don't access, read or copy that data.

Every data-bearing device is securely erased with certified data erasure software, or physically destroyed, following National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) guidance, and you receive a certificate confirming it. Read more about how we erase data.

For this data, your organisation remains the data controller and we act as your processor, handling it only to destroy it. Please back up anything you need before we collect your equipment, as erased data can't be recovered.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • be told how we use your information (this notice)
  • get a copy of the information we hold about you
  • have inaccurate information corrected
  • have your information deleted
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • receive your information in a format you can take elsewhere
  • withdraw your consent at any time, for example by unsubscribing from our emails.

To use any of these rights, call us on 07904010708 or write to us at the address above. We'll reply within one month, and there's usually no charge.

10. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first so we can put it right.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator: make a complaint on the ICO website or call the ICO helpline on 0303 123 1113.
