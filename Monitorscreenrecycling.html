We accept a wide range of displays, including LCD, LED, ultrawide, touchscreen, digital signage and large-format advertising screens. Whether you're upgrading office workstations, refurbishing retail premises or replacing commercial display systems, we can help manage the disposal process.

Sustainable Monitor Recycling

Monitors contain valuable materials that can be recovered and reused. Through responsible recycling, components such as plastics, circuit boards, metals, cables and glass can be separated and processed for material recovery, helping reduce electronic waste and conserve natural resources.

Where suitable, working monitors may also be prioritised for reuse, extending their lifespan and reducing the need for new equipment.

Frequently asked questions

Is monitor and screen recycling free?

Working displays up to 30 inches can be recycled free of charge. Larger displays and screens that are damaged may incur a recycling charge.

Are there charges for broken or larger screens?

Yes. A cracked or broken display up to 30 inches is charged at £10. Displays over 30 inches are charged at £1 per inch, whether working or damaged. Interactive whiteboards and smartboards are also charged at £1 per inch, regardless of condition.

Do you accept CRT monitors?

No. We do not currently collect CRT screens because they require specialist handling due to the materials they contain.

What types of monitors can you recycle?

We accept LCD, TFT, LED, OLED, curved, ultrawide, 4K, touchscreen and professional broadcast monitors, as well as commercial advertising and merchandising displays, regardless of brand.

What happens to monitors after collection?

We first assess equipment for potential reuse. Monitors that cannot be reused are sent for responsible recycling, where materials such as plastics, circuit boards and cables can be recovered.

Do you provide certificates for monitor recycling?

Yes. You'll receive a Certificate of Destruction and a certificate of carbon offset for the collection, and enhanced data destruction with detailed audit logs is available on request.

Can monitors be collected with other IT equipment?

Yes. We can collect monitors together with computers, laptops, printers and other IT equipment, making it easier to clear multiple types of equipment in one collection.
